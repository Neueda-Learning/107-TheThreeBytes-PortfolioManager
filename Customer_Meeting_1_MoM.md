## 3. Customer Requirements

- Provide financial information for all supported assets.
- Separate dashboards for each asset class.
- Combined portfolio dashboard.
- Display live daily market prices.
- Show stock allocation percentages.
- Display selling value, investment risk, and holding duration.
- Estimate taxes based on:
  - Short-term capital gains
  - Long-term capital gains
  - Old tax regime
  - New tax regime

- Dividend Tracking
- Risk Analysis

---

## 5. Decisions

- Initial asset classes will include Stocks, Bonds, and Cryptocurrency.
- Dashboard will support both individual asset views and a combined portfolio view.
- Tax estimation will be included as a core feature.
- Live market data APIs will be evaluated before implementation.
